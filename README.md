# Hive Sight

Hive Sight makes bee hives easier to read. Vanilla hives are fine, but you can't tell how full one is without breaking it, you can't get a bee out until it decides to leave, and the honey only shows on the front. Hive Sight smooths those edges without changing what hives are. Everything here applies to crafted bee hives and natural bee nests alike.

## Features

- Hold shift with your crosshair on a hive and two lines appear under the crosshair: how many bees are inside, out of three, and the honey level, out of five. Let go of shift, look away, or step out of reach and they're gone.
- Shift-right-click a hive to let one bee out, whatever you're holding. Plain right-clicks with shears or a bottle still harvest as usual; with shift held they eject instead. It works at night and in the rain. Click again for the next bee. An empty hive gives a dry click and a puff of smoke.
- When a hive is full, the honey drips show on all four sides instead of only the front.

Each feature has its own switch under Mod Menu, Hive Sight.

## Install

- MenuKit 2.0.0 or newer
- Fabric API

Install on both client and server. In single player nothing else is needed.

## Compatibility

Minecraft 26.2, Fabric. On a dedicated server, the eject switch is the server's setting, not each player's. The Honey Faces switch reloads resources when you flip it, which takes a moment. Resource packs that retexture hives keep their front, top, and bottom; the two honey side textures come from Hive Sight.
