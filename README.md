# Hive Sight

Bee hives made legible. Vanilla hives are charming but opaque: you can't tell at a glance how full one is, and you can't get a bee out without waiting. Hive Sight smooths those edges without changing what hives are. Everything applies to crafted bee hives and natural bee nests alike.

## Features

Hold shift with your crosshair on a hive and a small card appears below the crosshair with the bee count and the honey level. Let go, look away, or step out of reach and it disappears.

Shift-right-click a hive to let one bee out, whatever you are holding. Shears and bottles do their normal thing on a plain right-click; with shift held they eject instead. It works at night and in the rain. Click again for the next bee. An empty hive gives a dry click and a puff of smoke.

Each feature has its own toggle under Mod Menu, Hive Sight.

## Requirements

- MenuKit 2.0.0 or newer
- Fabric API

Install on both client and server. On a dedicated server, the eject toggle is the server's setting.
